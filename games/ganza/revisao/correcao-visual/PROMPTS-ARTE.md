# Prompts de produção da correção pastel

Ferramenta utilizada: **ImageGen integrado**, quatro gerações com referências. Masters guardados em `../../art-src/`. Não foi usado o fallback CLI/API.

## Paisagens horizontais

Referência: `../lofi-progressao-4-volatilidades.png`.

> Production game environment atlas, strict borderless 2 by 2 grid of four equal landscape panels. Approved reference supplies the painterly LoFi gouache style, sunset light, vivid pastel palette and Moroccan progression. Remove all people, pins, boats, lettering and UI. Every panel has lavender-pink sunset sky and round moon, a white Moroccan village on cliffs at the left edge, clear turquoise sea in the center and right, and a low foreground terrace with cushions, rugs and bougainvillea. Calm coast in the first panel. Add brass lanterns and zellige in the second. Add a richer medina and terracotta arch in the third. Add a psychedelic scalloped rainbow pastel arch framing the edges in the fourth. Keep central water clear for live gameplay. Same horizon and terrace geometry. No opaque triangle, cream borders, text or watermark.

Master: `../../art-src/coasts-pastel-v3.png`.

## WEED sentado

Referências de identidade: as duas fotografias autorizadas em `../../../piece-police/INSPIRATION/WEED/`. Referência de pintura e pose: progressão LoFi.

> Identity-preserving transparent PNG animation atlas, 4 columns by 2 rows, eight equal square cells. The same adult man in every cell: bucket hat, elongated face, hazel eyes, fine mustache and short pointed goatee, matching both identity photographs. Full seated body, charcoal tracksuit with jade stripes and Moroccan embroidery, bent knees, elbow resting on knee, feet visible. Warm golden sunset gouache brushwork, pastel color blocking, never a photographic face pasted onto paint. Consistent framing, scale, head height, seat and feet anchors. Eight subtle successive poses: relaxed hand at chest, lifting hand, cigarette near lips, quiet puff with lower eyelids, lowering hand and exhaling, pleased smile, looking at the water, returning to relaxed pose. No smoke baked into atlas. Clean transparent alpha, no fringe, no cushions, floor, frame, grid, text or watermark.

Master: `../../art-src/weed-seated-v3.png`.

## Lanchas

Referência de estilo: progressão LoFi.

> Transparent PNG sprite atlas, four equal wide horizontal cells stacked in one column. One black rigid inflatable Moroccan smuggling speedboat per cell, side three-quarter view, bow right, twin outboard motors, black rubber tube, small console and windshield. Painterly LoFi gouache with warm sunset light, teal hull shadows and golden glints. Same framing and scale. First hull plain black, second subtle jade zellige, third ornate gold and turquoise mosaic, fourth rose-violet turquoise mosaic glow. Painted foam and turquoise reflection fade to transparent below each hull. Empty central hull area for runtime labels. No people, text, numbers, UI, border or rectangular water patch. Clean alpha.

Master: `../../art-src/boats-pastel-v3.png`.
